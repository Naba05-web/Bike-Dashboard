# Bike-Dashboard
Bike Sales Dashboard (Excel)
** Overview

I built this dashboard to find out what kinds of customers are most likely to buy a bike. Using Excel, I cleaned up a dataset from Kaggle, pulled the details together, and designed an interactive dashboard that lets anyone explore the data.

** What I Did

Cleaned the raw data (removed duplicates, fixed missing info, grouped ages, and standardized income/commute distance).

Used VLOOKUP to combine customer details like age, gender, income, education, and marital status with purchase history.

Built Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts to show patterns across income, age, commute distance, and region.

Designed a dashboard with slicers so users can filter by things like income, education, and region to see the trends for themselves.

** What I Found

Middle-aged customers are the most likely to buy a bike.

Higher-income individuals ($30k+) are more likely to purchase.

People with a 1–2 mile commute are the strongest target group.

** Why It Matters

Instead of looking at raw data, the dashboard makes it easy to spot buying patterns at a glance. It helps answer questions like “Who should we market bikes to?” in a way that’s simple and visual.
